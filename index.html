V. / BACKGROUND

Two disciplines.
One practice.

I earned a B.A. in Philosophy, with distinction, from the University of Rochester (2015), with particular interests in German Idealism, Martin Heidegger, and logic. I subsequently earned an M.S. in Data Science from National University (2023).

Professionally, I work as a Data Scientist in healthcare IT, bringing together software development, data engineering, and analytical methods to solve operationally consequential problems.

My professional experience includes data science and healthcare information systems at Brattleboro Retreat, Brattleboro Memorial Hospital, and Tufts Medicine, as well as teaching as an adjunct faculty member at Vermont State University. I develop data-intensive applications and offer consulting for organizations that need rigorous thinking translated into working technology.
